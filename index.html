Curiosity is usually where my projects begin.

I'm Luigi, a BSIT student exploring web development, programming, game development, networking, and creative technology.

I like building things that interest me: a website, a game, an animation, or an idea I've never tried before. Most of the time I start without knowing exactly how to make it. I learn as I go, experiment, mess things up, and try again.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, but that's what I enjoy about it. Every project gives me something new to learn, and every new thing I learn gives me something else I want to build.

This portfolio is a collection of what I'm learning and the things I'm interested in.
