## Libraries, fonts, and services

- [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/) 7.3.0, loaded from cdnjs for interface and brand icons. Review the [Font Awesome license](https://fontawesome.com/license/free), including any brand-icon requirements.
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128, loaded from cdnjs for the red theme background. Review its [license](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE).
- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) — JetBrains Mono, Space Grotesk, VT323, and Silkscreen.
- [CDNFonts](https://www.cdnfonts.com/) — Ghost Stories Deluxe and Poppins. The project does not record font author or individual license details; confirm the applicable terms.
- [Lanyard](https://github.com/Phineas/lanyard) public API and the [Discord CDN](https://discord.com/) are used to display the linked Discord account's public presence and avatar data.
